Lead Scraping Platform

A web platform that turns "we need more clients" into a living database of qualified prospects — built to feed sales pipelines automatically and cut manual prospecting to zero.

You enter a target niche and location. The engine crawls business directories, map listings, and public websites, extracting company names, phone numbers, emails, and websites.
